# Security training & culture

Security maturity is sustained by habits, not just policies.

## Program principles

1. Make training continuous and practical.
2. Tailor content by role, access level, and threat exposure.
3. Measure behavior change (reporting rates, simulation outcomes, control adoption).

## Personal OpSec expectations for team members

1. Manage digital footprint and privacy settings on personal accounts.
2. Avoid oversharing role details, travel location, or sensitive context publicly.
3. Use strong security on personal devices used for work (updates, encryption, password manager, MFA).
4. Segment home networks and isolate untrusted devices.
5. Separate personal and professional identities/channels where possible.

## Web3-specific people risks

1. Avoid publicly linking personal identities to high-value on-chain assets.
2. Separate personal and operational wallets.
3. Reassess personal threat models as role visibility and access change.

## Culture

1. Reward early reporting and transparent escalation.
2. Treat “verify before trust” as normal behavior.
3. Keep leadership visibly engaged in security routines.

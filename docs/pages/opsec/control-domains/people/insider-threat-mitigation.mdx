# Insider threat mitigation

Insider risk includes malicious actions, negligent behavior, and compromised accounts used by external adversaries.

## Detection

1. Monitor for unusual access patterns, privilege use, and data movement.
2. Baseline normal behavior and alert on high-risk deviations.
3. Review role changes and dormant privileged accounts.

## Prevention

1. Enforce least privilege and separation of duties.
2. Run recurring anti-phishing and social-engineering training.
3. Apply stronger controls to high-value systems (MFA, approvals, dual control).
4. Use structured onboarding/offboarding with immediate access revocation on departure.

## Cultural and process controls

1. Establish clear reporting channels for suspicious behavior and coercion attempts.
2. Include insider scenarios in incident response playbooks.
3. Conduct periodic access and entitlement reviews with system owners.

## Web3 context

In Web3 operations, compromised insiders can directly impact key management, validator operations, or treasury controls. Prioritize controls around signing authority and recovery paths.

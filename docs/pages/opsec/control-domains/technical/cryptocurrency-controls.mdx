# Cryptocurrency controls

Web3 operations require controls that account for irreversible transactions and key-centric risk.

## Baseline controls

1. Separate treasury, operations, and personal wallets.
2. Use hardware-backed key management for high-value assets.
3. Enforce multi-party approval for sensitive transfers.
4. Keep signer devices hardened and operationally isolated.

## Operational safety

1. Define transaction verification and change-management procedures.
2. Use allowlists, simulation, and pre-signing review for complex operations.
3. Maintain break-glass and incident-response procedures for key compromise.

## Visibility and governance

1. Inventory wallets, contracts, and privileges.
2. Monitor on-chain activity for anomalous behavior.
3. Review signer roles and permissions on a recurring cadence.

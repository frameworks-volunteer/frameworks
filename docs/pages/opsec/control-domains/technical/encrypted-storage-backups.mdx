# Encrypted storage & backups

Data protection requires lifecycle controls across storage, transfer, and recovery.

## Core practices

1. Classify data by sensitivity and business impact.
2. Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit.
3. Minimize retained data and define retention/deletion windows.

## Backups and recovery

1. Maintain regular backups for critical systems and data.
2. Protect backups with encryption and access controls.
3. Store copies in separate failure domains.
4. Test restoration regularly and document recovery time objectives.

## Operational controls

1. Implement secure sharing paths for sensitive material.
2. Reduce exfiltration risk with logging, policy controls, and review.
3. Periodically reassess controls against evolving threats and business requirements.

## Web3 considerations

Protect cryptographic secrets, signing artifacts, and recovery material with stronger handling requirements than ordinary business data.

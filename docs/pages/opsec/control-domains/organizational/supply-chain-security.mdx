# Supply chain security

Third-party and vendor dependencies can become primary attack paths.

## Vendor governance baseline

1. Inventory critical vendors and the systems/data they can affect.
2. Perform risk-tiered due diligence before onboarding.
3. Define security and incident-notification requirements contractually.
4. Restrict vendor access to least privilege and required time windows.

## Ongoing assurance

1. Reassess high-risk vendors on a fixed cadence and after major incidents.
2. Monitor external security posture signals and dependency advisories.
3. Require timely patching and remediation for identified risks.

## Failure preparedness

1. Maintain contingency plans for vendor outage/compromise.
2. Test revocation and failover paths.
3. Ensure internal ownership for each critical vendor relationship.

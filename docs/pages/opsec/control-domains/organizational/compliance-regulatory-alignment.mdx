# Compliance & regulatory alignment

Compliance should reinforce security outcomes, not replace them.

## Operating model

1. Map controls to applicable laws, contractual obligations, and internal policies.
2. Assign control ownership and review cadence.
3. Keep evidence collection lightweight but consistent.

## Practical controls

1. Maintain clear security policies and role responsibilities.
2. Align incident response, access control, and data protection practices with regulatory expectations.
3. Track exceptions and compensating controls with explicit risk acceptance.

## Continuous alignment

1. Reassess control coverage when systems, vendors, or jurisdictions change.
2. Use post-incident and audit findings to improve the control set.
3. Ensure policy updates are communicated and enforced operationally.
